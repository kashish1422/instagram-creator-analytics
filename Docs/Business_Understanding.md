### 1. Project Objective:
Building an analytis system that helps an Instagram creator understand content performance, audience growth, engagement, and content strategy using historical Instagram data.

The project aims to identify what is happening with the creator's performance, which factors are associated with stronger performance, and where there are opportunities for improvement.

### 2. Business Problem:

Instagram creators generate large amounts of performance data, including likes, comments, views, reach, saves, shares, and follower data. Raw metrics alone do not clearly explain why some content performs better than others.

- The creator needs a structured analytics system to:

- Track account growth

- Understand content performance

- Compare different content formats

- Identify high-performing content

- Analyze engagement

- Understand posting patterns

- Identify relationships between content characteristics and performance

- Make data-informed content decisions

### 3. Stakeholders:

#### Primary Stakeholder

- Instagram Creator

#### Secondary Stakeholders

- Social Media Manager

- Content Strategist

- Marketing Manager

- Brand Partnership Manager

### 4. Business Questions:

#### 4.1 Audience & Growth

- How do follower gains vary over time?
- Which weeks or months have the highest recorded follower gains?
- Are there periods when average or total follower gains decrease?

#### 4.2 Content Performance

- Which media type has the highest average engagement and reach?
- Which individual posts have the highest engagement?
- Which posts receive the most shares and saves?
- What percentage of posts fall into each engagement-performance category?

#### 4.3 Engagement

- What is the average calculated engagement rate?
- How does engagement rate vary over time?
- Which media types and content categories have higher average engagement?
- Is reach associated with total engagement?

#### 4.4 Posting Strategy

- Which days of the week have higher average engagement?
- Which posting hours are associated with higher average engagement and reach?
- Is posting frequency associated with engagement or follower gains at the account level?

#### 4.5 Content Characteristics

- Which content categories have higher average engagement and follower gains?
- How do image, carousel, and reel performance differ?
- Is caption length associated with engagement? 

#### 4.6 Conversion

- Is the presence of a CTA associated with differences in average engagement?
- Does hashtag count relate to engagement? 
- Which content categories or media types are associated with higher recorded follower gains?

#### 4.7 Follower Growth

- Which media types and content categories have the highest average recorded follower gains?
- Is post engagement associated with follower gains?
- How do follower gains vary across traffic sources?

The business questions have been refined based on the selected dataset and the analysis performed. Questions requiring unavailable metrics or additional analysis are outside the current scope and may be considered for future project extensions.

### 5. KPIs:

The initial KPI list is:


- Total Followers: Measure audience size

- Follower Growth: Measure audience expansion

- Reach: Measure unique accounts reached

- Impressions: Measure total content exposure

- Views: Measure content consumption 

- Likes: Measure basic engagement

- Comments: Measure deeper engagement

- Shares: Measure content distribution

- Saves: Measure content value/interest

- Total Engagement: Measure overall interaction

- Engagement Rate :Measure engagement relative to reach/audience

- Profile Visits: Measure interest in the creator

- Follows from Content: Measure conversion

- Posting Frequency: Measure content consistency

- Average Reach/Post: Measure content reach efficiency

#### Total Engagement:

Total Engagement
= Likes + Comments + Shares + Saves

#### Engagement Rate:

Initial definition:

Engagement Rate
= Total Engagement / Reach × 100

The final formula will depend on the available dataset and will be documented clearly.
