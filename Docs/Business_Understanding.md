### 1. Project Objective:
Building an analytis system that helps an Instagram creator understand content performance, audience growth, engagement, and content strategy using historical Instagram data.

The project aims to identify what is happening with the creator's performance, which factors are associated with stronger performance, and where there are opportunities for improvement.

### 2. Business Problem:

Instagram creators generate large amounts of performance data, including likes, comments, views, reach, saves, shares, and follower data. Raw metrics alone do not clearly explain why some content performs better than others.

- The creator needs a structured analytics system to:

- Track account growth

- Understand content performance

- Compare different content formats

- Identify high-performing content

- Analyze engagement

- Understand posting patterns

- Identify relationships between content characteristics and performance

- Make data-informed content decisions

### 3. Stakeholders:

#### Primary Stakeholder

- Instagram Creator

#### Secondary Stakeholders

- Social Media Manager

- Content Strategist

- Marketing Manager

- Brand Partnership Manager

### 4. Business Questions:

#### 4.1 Audience & Growth

- How has follower count changed over time?
- What is the weekly/monthly follower growth?
- Which periods experienced the highest growth?
- Are there periods of declining growth?

#### 4.2 Content Performance

- Which content format performs best?
- Which posts/reels generate the highest reach?
- Which content generates the most engagement?
- Which content receives the most shares and saves?
- What percentage of content is high-performing?

#### 4.3 Engagement

- What is the average engagement rate?
- How does engagement rate change over time?
- Which content types have higher engagement?
- Is higher reach associated with higher engagement?

#### 4.4 Posting Strategy

- Which days are associated with better performance?
- Which posting times are associated with stronger performance?
- Does posting frequency relate to follower growth or engagement?

#### 4.5 Content Characteristics

- Which topics/content categories perform better?
- Do different caption lengths perform differently?
- Do Reels, carousels, images, and other formats perform differently?

#### 4.6 Conversion

- Does the number of hastags relate to content performance?
- Does caption length relate to engagement?
- Does having a call-to-action relate to engagement?
- Which content is most effective at converting viewers into followers?

#### 4.7 Follower Growth

- Which content characteristics are associated with higher follower gains?
- Is follower count associated with the number of followers gained from posts?
- Which content categories generate the highest follower gains?


Questions will be finalized after the dataset is selected. We will not create analysis for metrics that are unavailable in the dataset.

### 5. KPIs:

The initial KPI list is:


- Total Followers: Measure audience size

- Follower Growth: Measure audience expansion

- Reach: Measure unique accounts reached

- Impressions: Measure total content exposure

- Views: Measure content consumption 

- Likes: Measure basic engagement

- Comments: Measure deeper engagement

- Shares: Measure content distribution

- Saves: Measure content value/interest

- Total Engagement: Measure overall interaction

- Engagement Rate :Measure engagement relative to reach/audience

- Profile Visits: Measure interest in the creator

- Follows from Content: Measure conversion

- Posting Frequency: Measure content consistency

- Average Reach/Post: Measure content reach efficiency

#### Total Engagement:

Total Engagement
= Likes + Comments + Shares + Saves

#### Engagement Rate:

Initial definition:

Engagement Rate
= Total Engagement / Reach × 100

The final formula will depend on the available dataset and will be documented clearly.
